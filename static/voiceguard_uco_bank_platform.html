UNDERSTANDING THE VOICE THREATS
What is an AI Voice Clone?
A fake voice created by computer software using artificial intelligence. Fraudsters feed it a few seconds of a real customer's voice, and it can speak any script to sound exactly like them.
What is a Replay Attack?
This is when a fraudster records a customer's genuine phone call (e.g. using a hidden microphone or call recorder) and plays it back over the phone to the bank to fool us.
How does VoiceGuard detect fakes?
It checks the audio for hidden clues that the human ear cannot hear:
• AI Speech Checks: Computers make speech that sounds perfect but lacks the tiny, natural tremors and breathing sounds of real human voices.
• Replay Checks: Playing a recording back over a phone speaker introduces background hums, speaker echoes, and flat noise patterns.
What does the Risk Score mean?
It shows how likely the voice is to be fake:
• 0% – 20% (Safe): The voice sounds natural and authentic. Proceed normally.
• 21% – 79% (Caution): Some anomalies were found. Ask secondary security questions (e.g. last deposit, date of birth) before continuing.
• 80% – 100% (High Risk): Extremely likely to be fake. Stop! Do not authorize any transactions.
What should I tell the caller if fraud is suspected?
Do not alert the caller or sound hostile. Calmly state: "For your security, our system requires a quick secondary verification to complete this request. I am sending an OTP to your registered phone." If they refuse, freeze the transfer and notify your manager.
