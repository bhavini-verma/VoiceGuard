FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
What is a replay attack?
A replay attack occurs when a fraudster records a genuine customer's voice and replays it to bypass voice authentication. VoiceGuard detects replay attacks by analyzing temporal inconsistencies, background noise patterns, and recording artifacts that are present in previously-captured audio.
What is AI voice cloning?
AI voice cloning uses deep learning models (TTS/VC systems) to synthesize a replica of a person's voice using as little as 3–5 seconds of training audio. The cloned voice can be used to deceive voice authentication systems. VoiceGuard detects unnatural spectral patterns, phase inconsistencies, and missing biological micro-variations characteristic of AI-generated speech.
How does VoiceGuard work?
VoiceGuard uses a dual-engine Fusion Architecture: (1) Biological Feature Engine — extracts 349 features including jitter, shimmer, HNR, pitch variation, and prosody. (2) Deep Learning Engine — uses Wav2Vec2 to extract 2048 embeddings. Both engines feed into a Fusion Core that generates a final fraud probability score and risk classification.
What does the confidence score mean?
The confidence score represents how certain the model is about its prediction. A score of 95%+ means the model has very high certainty. Scores between 70–94% indicate moderate confidence — additional verification is recommended. Below 70% suggests an uncertain result, and manual review by a human expert is advisable.
How is replay detection performed?
Replay detection analyzes: (1) Background noise consistency, (2) Microphone channel artifacts from re-recording, (3) Unnatural silence patterns, (4) Temporal repetition signatures, and (5) Spectral smoothness anomalies. The system assigns a replay probability score separate from AI voice detection.
What are jitter, shimmer, and HNR?
These are biological voice features: Jitter — cycle-to-cycle frequency variation in vocal folds (higher = unnatural). Shimmer — amplitude variation between consecutive glottal pulses. HNR (Harmonics-to-Noise Ratio) — ratio of harmonic energy to noise in the signal. Synthetic voices exhibit abnormal values in all three because they lack true biological vocal tract dynamics.
RBI / BANK SOP GUIDANCE
Following RBI Circular No. RBI/2023-24/73 — Cyber Security Framework for Voice Fraud
When Customer Reports Voice Fraud:
1
Verify customer identity — Use alternate channel (video KYC or OTP) before taking any action.
2
Freeze transaction if necessary — Immediately pause any pending financial transaction linked to the fraudulent call.
3
Escalate to Fraud Team — Open an investigation ticket and assign to the Cyber Fraud Investigation Cell.
4
Preserve evidence — Save the original audio file, call logs, and VoiceGuard report as per CERT-In guidelines.
5
Notify the customer — Inform the customer of the finding and provide guidance on securing their account.
6
File regulatory report — Report to NPCI/RBI within 6 hours if transaction value exceeds ₹1 lakh, per CERT-In mandate.
7
Retrain model — Submit the confirmed fraud case as training feedback to improve VoiceGuard's detection accuracy.
